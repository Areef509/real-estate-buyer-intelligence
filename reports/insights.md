# Business Insights

> Statements about *needs* and *friction* are **hypotheses derived from observed segment behaviour** (not measured preferences) and should be validated with customer research. Segment findings describe groups, never individuals.

## Segment 0: Long-Horizon Buyers (27% of buyers)

- Office purchases account for 8% of units vs 15% overall.
- Purchases are spread over 19 months on average vs 14 overall.
- 36% of this segment applied for a loan vs 37% overall — no material difference.
- 31% acquired for investment vs 31% overall — no material difference.
- Average satisfaction is 3.01/5 vs 3.03 overall.

**Needs:** No segment-specific need is supported by the data; use individual signals.

**Friction:** No segment-specific friction is supported by the data.

**Marketing:** Mixed content: the investment/home split matches the overall population, so let each client's own stated purpose steer the message.

**Property:** Match recommendations to the observed price point (segment average $362,424). Typical unit size is about 1178 sq ft at $308 per sq ft. Prioritise apartments; office units are less common in this segment's purchases.

**Financing:** Loan usage (36%) does not differ materially from the overall rate (37%): decide financing messaging per client from the client's own loan flag, not from the segment.

**Engagement:** Satisfaction is in line with overall; monitor low individual scores for service recovery. 40% of the segment rated satisfaction 1-2.

- Purchase activity is lower than other buyers: 3.4 purchases on average vs 3.7 overall.
- Average purchase value is higher: $376,351 vs $347,090 overall (observed transactions only).
- Office purchases account for 39% of units vs 15% overall.
- Purchases are spread over 12 months on average vs 14 overall.
- 35% of this segment applied for a loan vs 37% overall — no material difference.

**Financing:** Loan usage (35%) does not differ materially from the overall rate (37%): decide financing messaging per client from the client's own loan flag, not from the segment.

**Engagement:** Keep communication focused on the specific listings already viewed. Satisfaction is in line with overall; monitor low individual scores for service recovery. 40% of the segment rated satisfaction 1-2.

## Segment 4: High-Volume Long-Horizon Buyers (6% of buyers)

- Purchase activity is higher than other buyers: 6.0 purchases on average vs 3.7 overall.
- Average purchase value is lower: $325,992 vs $347,090 overall (observed transactions only).

**Engagement:** Offer a single point of contact and pre-filled details for follow-on purchases. Invite feedback and optional referrals; avoid over-contacting a satisfied group. 29% of the segment rated satisfaction 1-2.
